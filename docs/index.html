tldw

Privacy Policy

tldw is a browser extension that turns the videos on a page — YouTube, Vimeo, or any native HTML5 video — into structured Markdown summaries. It runs entirely in your browser with your own API keys. Source code: github.com/adrnbttr/tldw.

Short version

There is no backend under our control. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your data anywhere. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party telemetry.

What is stored, and where

Everything lives in chrome.storage.local, on your machine only: your API keys, your preferences, and a local history of the summaries you generate (a configurable limit, default 50). You can clear it at any time by removing the extension.

Where your data goes

Data Sent to When
Video transcript text OpenRouter (your API key) When you generate a summary
Extracted audio Your transcription provider (your API key) Only when the audio fallback runs
API keys Only the provider they belong to On each request to that provider

These are your own accounts with those providers; their handling of the data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

What tldw never does

  • Never transmits your keys to anyone but the matching provider.
  • Never accesses the contents of cross-origin iframes — only the iframe src is read to identify the video.
  • Never attempts to bypass DRM.
  • Never runs analytics or contacts a server we operate.

Your responsibility

Extracting content behind authentication may be governed by a site's terms of use. Confirming that your usage is permitted is your responsibility. tldw is intended for personal use on content you legitimately have access to.
